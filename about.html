From the field to the toolbox

Steve started out apprenticing under a local contractor, learning carpentry, plumbing, electrical basics, and finish work from the ground up. Twenty years later, he still shows up on time, still double-checks his work, and still won't leave a job half-finished.

Today, Steve runs a one-man operation by design. When you call, you talk to the person who's actually doing the work — not a dispatcher, not a sales rep. That means fewer miscommunications, more accountability, and pricing that doesn't have a middleman markup baked in.

Why neighbors call Steve back

Handyman work runs on trust — someone in your home, around your family, handling things that matter. Steve treats every house like it's his own: shoes off, floors covered, mess cleaned up before he leaves.
